FAQ

Questions, answered.

The things homeowners ask before they build. Still curious? Call (385) 600-6240 or get a free quote.

How much does a Petrified fence cost?
Fences are priced by the linear foot and depend on the style you choose, the number of corners, and freight to your site. As a rough guide, our privacy fence typically runs $88 to $104 per linear foot installed. Every quote is free and specific to your property. Get a free quote.
Is it really concrete?
Yes. Every panel is solid concrete, stamped with a real wood-grain texture and hand-stained. It only looks like wood.
Will it really look like wood?
From the street, most people assume it is a premium wood fence. The grain, knots, and board lines are pulled from real timber. Up close, it has the weight and permanence of stone.
Does it need any maintenance?
A little. Concrete won't rot, warp, or burn, but to keep your limited lifetime warranty in force the fence should be resealed by Petrified or an approved installation partner every two years. Between reseals, an occasional rinse is all it takes.
Is it fire resistant?
Concrete is non-combustible, so unlike wood or vinyl it does not burn. It is a strong choice in wildfire-prone areas.
How does it hold up in wind and storms?
With proper installation it is engineered to stand through 115 mph wind.
Is it a better value than a wood fence?
Up front it costs more than a wood fence. Over time it can cost less, because you're not replacing rotted or storm-damaged boards. Ask us for a project-specific quote.
What areas do you serve?
We currently serve projects throughout Utah and Idaho, and we're seeking installation and distribution partners in surrounding states. Outside Utah or Idaho? Contact us to check availability.
Are you a manufacturer or an installer?
We manufacture the concrete fencing and work directly with homeowners on quotes and planning. Installation is handled by our team or an established local partner, depending on your project and location.
How do I get started?
Request a free quote or call (385) 600-6240. We'll talk through your project, measure where possible, and prepare a tailored quote.
How long does installation take?
Most residential projects install in a few days, depending on the length and your site. We give you a timeline with your quote.
Can it follow a slope or uneven ground?
Yes. Our panel-and-post system steps to follow the grade of your property.
What finishes are available?
Four wood-tone finishes: Mahogany, Redwood, Hickory, and Driftwood, from warm red-browns to a weathered grey.
Do you offer gates?
Not at this time. We focus on premium concrete fence panels, posts, and rails.
What does the warranty cover?
Every Petrified fence is backed by a limited lifetime warranty. We will go over the full written terms with your quote.
Will it pass my HOA?
Often, yes, and we are happy to provide product specs and photos to include in your HOA submission.
Is it eco-friendly?
It uses no cut lumber and needs no chemical stains or sealers over its life, so there is nothing to reapply and nothing to send to a landfill in a few years.
How do I get started?
Request a free quote or call (385) 600-6240. We will visit, measure, and give you a no-pressure estimate, usually within one business day. Get a free quote.
